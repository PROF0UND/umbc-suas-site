Avionics safety: 30s

Reliability starts with our wiring. Every servo extension routed outside the fuselage is soldered rather than relying on friction-fit connectors, eliminating in-flight disconnects from vibration or airflow. Inside the fuselage, all batteries and avionics are secured with Velcro retention, so nothing shifts under maneuvering loads or changes our CG mid-flight. Our telemetry radio is belly-mounted for unobstructed line of sight to the ground station. And we built a custom antenna tracker that actively follows the aircraft - giving us reliable telemetry at long range throughout the mission.
